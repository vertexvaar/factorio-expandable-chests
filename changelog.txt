Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-03
  Features:
    - Chest linker tool (shortcut "Link chests", control can be bound in the settings): drag over chests to link all neighbours of the same type and quality, shift-drag to cut every side of the selected chests.
    - Only one chest of a group reports to each circuit network; the others stop reading so the contents are not counted twice, and read again when the group is split or the reader is removed.
    - Flying texts tell when chests stop or start reporting to a circuit network, when requests are dropped and when items had to be put on the ground.
  Changes:
    - When two logistic chests link, one that requests something stays the main chest.
  Bugfixes:
    - "Read empty slots" and its signal are kept when a chest joins or leaves a group.
    - Splitting big groups is much faster.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-23
  Features:
    - Link neighbouring chests of the same type into one shared inventory, side by side in the chest window.
    - Works for wooden, iron and steel chests, all logistic chests and 1x1 chests from other mods.
    - Unlocked by its own technology at the same tier and cost as fluid handling.
    - Links survive blueprints, fast replace and upgrade planners; removed chests take their share of the items.
    - Warns when several chests of a group report to the same circuit network and lets one chest report alone.
